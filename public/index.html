程明，音樂學者、錄音室吉他手。1995 年起活躍於臺灣音樂產業，2026 年取得國立臺灣大學音樂學博士，現任中央研究院資訊科學研究所音樂與文化科技實驗室博士後研究學者，以社會網絡分析、大型語言模型與深度學習方法研究戰後臺灣流行音樂的錄音室幕後。Ming Cheng is a musicologist and session guitarist. Active in Taiwan’s music industry since 1995, he received his Ph.D. in musicology from National Taiwan University in 2026 and is now a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Music and Culture Technology Lab, Institute of Information Science, Academia Sinica, studying the recording-studio backstage of postwar Taiwanese popular music with social network analysis, large language models, and deep learning.
